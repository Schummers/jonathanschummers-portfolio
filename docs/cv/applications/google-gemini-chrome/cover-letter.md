# Cover letter — Google, UX Designer, Gemini in Chrome

> 16 June 2026 · English · 1 page A4 · direct application
> Role centers on "designing for non-deterministic outcomes and establishing
> trust between the user and AI" on desktop Chrome.

---

Dear Gemini in Chrome team,

I design AI products, and I use AI to design them. Over my last two roles I built my own skills for product discovery, visual ideation, and design-system compliance. AI changed how I learn, work, and think, and more generally what I thought was possible. I want to bring that to people who are not power users, by designing how AI can help them in their everyday interactions while browsing in Chrome.

At TotalEnergies Digital Factory, I designed two machine-learning products for refinery and power-plant operators. The hardest part was earning the operators' confidence through the interface: showing a prediction next to its confidence interval, explaining what drove the calculation, and layering the information so an operator could act at a glance or deep dive into the reasoning when the stakes were high. When a prediction was off, the expert could flag it, and that feedback routed back to the engineering team to improve the model. Adoption across 4 refineries cut pipe leaks 6%.

On Valoris, our research showed the friction of daily documentation through web forms for an individual landlord. So I moved the core experience into a chat assistant on the channels people already use (iMessage, Telegram), with agentic workflows that turn messy inputs and documents into structured data. Every extraction lands in a staging table first, and nothing reaches the real records until the user confirms; the assistant cannot edit or delete data from the chat, gated by predefined commands and scoped permissions. That work convinced me of where this goes next. Power users already live inside agentic tools like Antigravity 2.0, running web searches straight from the tool. The open problem is bringing that power to everyone else, natively in the browser they already use and wired into Google's ecosystem.

I would welcome the chance to design the agentic browsing experience with you.

Jonathan Schummers
