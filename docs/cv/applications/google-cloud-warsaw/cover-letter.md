Dear Cloud team,

I love designing solutions, and I love it even more when they ship. That is why I have always built a design culture with the engineering team, bringing them in early so we shape solutions together instead of handing over finished screens. A designer advocates for the user, but the real work is aligning those needs with product strategy and the stakeholders' vision, and making that case in their language, with research and data.

At TotalEnergies Digital Factory, I designed machine-learning products for refinery and power-plant operators. Because my POs and stakeholders were energy experts, not digital ones, I framed every epic around a user need and a business outcome, and argued for a chosen design against the alternative solutions. I backed those calls with usability tests and adoption data rather than opinion. The result: 500+ inspectors across 4 refineries, 82% daily active, pipe leaks down 6%.

On Valoris, I framed the core user problem from research, then presented side-by-side alternatives, web forms against a chat assistant, to steer the team's pivot. I ran discovery, design, and build solo, validating the call with real landlords and iterating on what the data showed.

AI changed how I learn, work, and think, and more generally what I thought was possible. Over my last two roles, I built my own custom skills for product discovery, visual ideation, and design-system compliance. The real shift was not speed but quality, mainly because it lets me explore, research, and iterate more from the get-go.

Jonathan Schummers
