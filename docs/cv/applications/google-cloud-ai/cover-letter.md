Dear Cloud AI team,

I design AI products, and I use AI to design them. Over my last two roles I built my own skills for product discovery, visual ideation, and design-system compliance. AI changed how I learn, work, and think, and more generally what I thought was possible. Now I want to help enterprises build, orchestrate, and collaborate with intelligent agents they can trust, which is the future your team is shaping.

At TotalEnergies Digital Factory, I designed two machine-learning products for refinery and power-plant operators. The hardest part was earning the operators' confidence through the interface: showing a prediction next to its confidence interval, explaining what drove the calculation, and layering the information so an operator could act at a glance or deep dive into the reasoning when the stakes were high. When a prediction was off, the expert could flag it, and that feedback routed back to the engineering team to improve the model. Adoption across 4 refineries cut pipe leaks 6%.

On Valoris, our research showed the friction of daily documentation through web forms for an individual landlord. So I moved the core experience into a chat assistant with agentic workflows that turn messy inputs and documents into structured data. Every step of autonomy buys speed and costs control, so the first decision is whether a task is even worth making non-deterministic. When the gain is worth the risk, the next one is how much oversight to design in, human in the loop or on it: every extraction lands in a staging table the user confirms before anything touches the real records, and the agent cannot edit or delete on its own. Fine-grained commands and permissions keep the user a reviewer who understands what the agent is doing, not someone trusting a black box.

These two products taught me to turn opaque technical logic, model predictions, data pipelines, and multi-step automation, into interfaces people trust and can correct. I would value the chance to build those foundational patterns and mental models for enterprise agents with you.

Jonathan Schummers
